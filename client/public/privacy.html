Privacy Policy

Matha Pacchi (माथा पच्ची)
Last updated: 1 September 2026
Matha Pacchi is designed to work offline. Your game data stays on your device.

Privacy at a Glance

Quick facts about how Matha Pacchi protects your privacy:

✓ No account
✓ No advertising
✓ No analytics
✓ No tracking
✓ No location tracking
✓ No internet required
✓ Local storage only

What Stays on Your Device

Matha Pacchi stores basic gameplay preferences locally on your phone so you can resume playing smoothly:

Game settings

Sound, haptics, chaos mode, tilt sensitivity, round length, and touch controls.
Why: To remember your preferred way to play.

High scores

Your best score for each deck.
Why: So you can track and beat your records.

Recently played decks

Your recently played content decks.
Why: So frequently played decks can appear first.
None of this data leaves your device.

Deleting the app permanently removes this locally stored data.

What Matha Pacchi Does Not Collect

The application does not collect, request, or store any of the following:

✕ Names
✕ Email addresses
✕ Phone numbers
✕ Device identifiers
✕ Location data
✕ Contacts
✕ Photos
✕ Camera data
✕ Microphone data
✕ Analytics data
✕ Advertising / tracking data

Matha Pacchi does not make background network requests or connect to external tracking servers during gameplay.

Motion Sensors

Matha Pacchi uses your phone's motion sensors while you are playing with the phone held against your forehead.

The sensors allow the game to detect gameplay actions in real-time:

Tilt forward → Correct
Tilt backward → Pass

Sensor readings are processed temporarily in memory during active rounds and are never recorded, logged, or stored to disk.

Technical Note: On Android, high-frequency motion sensor access requires the android.permission.HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS permission in the application manifest to ensure responsive tilt detection.

Sharing Your Score

When you tap Share on the results screen, Matha Pacchi creates a small text summary containing the relevant game result (such as the deck title, score achieved, and title earned).

The app passes that text summary directly to your device's normal system sharing interface. You choose whether to share it and which application or person receives it.

Matha Pacchi itself does not upload, publish, or transmit your game results anywhere.

Internet Connection

Matha Pacchi does not require an internet connection to play.

All 13 content decks and 947 cards are bundled directly inside the application package. You can play completely offline anywhere—on flights, road trips, or off-grid living rooms.
