Privacy Policy

Maatha Pacchi (माथा पच्ची) Android Application
Last updated: 4 September 2026

Notice: This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the Maatha Pacchi Android mobile application. For information regarding the games.adityagagare.in website infrastructure, see Section 14.

1. At a Glance

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide any personal details to play.
  • Zero Data Collection: The app does not collect, track, or transmit your personal data.
  • No Advertisements: The app contains no third-party ad networks or ad identifiers.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: There are no third-party tracking or crash-reporting SDKs installed.
  • 100% Local Gameplay: The app operates offline on your device and stores settings locally.

Maatha Pacchi is designed to be a self-contained party card game that respects user privacy.

The Maatha Pacchi Android application does not collect any personal information.

Specifically, the application does NOT collect or request:

  • Account credentials or social media profiles

  • Geographic location data (GPS or network-based)

  • Microphone audio recordings or camera images
  • Gameplay telemetry, analytics, or crash reports

4. Sensors and Device Features

Maatha Pacchi relies on your phone's hardware motion sensors to power hands-free tilt gameplay while holding the phone against your forehead.

Motion Sensors

The app uses expo-sensors (DeviceMotion / Accelerometer) and requests the Android permission HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS in the application build manifest.

  • Purpose: To calculate real-time phone tilt angle (pitch) during an active round so the game can register when you tilt the phone DOWN (for Correct) or UP (for Pass).
  • Data Handling: Sensor readings are processed temporarily in device memory during active rounds. Motion readings are never saved to disk, never logged, and never transmitted off the device.

Vibration Motor

The app requests the VIBRATE permission to trigger brief haptic vibration responses when a card is answered or passed. Haptics can be toggled on or off at any time in the app settings.

6. Internet and Network Access

All 13 content decks and 947 gameplay cards are bundled directly inside the application. The game does not connect to any remote server or external API to load cards or gameplay content.

Manifest Permission Note: The Android build configuration includes the standard android.permission.INTERNET permission (a default component of the Expo / React Native build toolchain). However, the application source code performs zero network requests, zero remote content fetching, and zero data uploads during normal gameplay operation.

7. Sharing Results

At the end of a round, you have the option to share your score card by tapping the "Share" button on the results screen.

  • User-Initiated: Sharing occurs only if and when you explicitly tap the Share button.
  • Android Share Sheet: Tapping Share hands a simple plain-text string (containing the deck title, score, and title earned) to Android's native system share menu.
  • User Choice: You choose which application to share the text with (such as messaging or social apps). Maatha Pacchi does not choose the recipient or transmit data independently. Any third-party app you share to will handle that text under its own privacy policy.

8. Third-Party Services

The Maatha Pacchi Android application does NOT integrate or bundle any third-party services that collect user data, including:

  • No advertising networks (e.g. AdMob, Unity Ads)
  • No analytics services (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
  • No crash-reporting services (e.g. Sentry, Crashlytics)
  • No Google Play Billing SDKs (currently not integrated)

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all app data (settings, high scores, recent decks) is stored strictly on your local device, we do not maintain central servers or databases containing your data.

How to Delete Stored Data:

  • Uninstall the Maatha Pacchi app from your Android device; OR
  • Go to Android Settings > Apps > Maatha Pacchi > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.

Performing either action instantly and permanently erases all locally saved settings and high scores.

10. Children's Privacy

Maatha Pacchi is a family-friendly party game designed for players of all ages.

Because the application does not collect, request, or transmit any personal information from any user, it does not collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under applicable local legal ages).

11. Security

Your gameplay data remains secure because it never leaves your physical device. We do not store or manage personal data on external web servers or cloud databases. Local preferences are stored safely within Android's sandboxed application data directory.
